Our Story

How We Met

Rey and Jona first met through a dating app. Neither was looking for anything serious, just hoping to pass the time — until a mutual swipe right set their story into motion. Realizing that simple app messages weren't enough, Rey asked for Jona's Facebook account so they could truly get to know each other.

Fate seemed determined to bring them together from the start. On the very day they began chatting, Rey accidentally dropped his phone into a bucket of water, while Jona had only just returned to the app after taking a break. Against the odds of ruined electronics and precise timing, destiny bridged the gap between them.

That chance match grew into a deep, genuine bond filled with shared moments and constant support. Today, surrounded by the people who mean the most to them, Rey and Jona are excited to take the next step and finally begin their journey as husband and wife.

The Proposal

On October 4, 2025, Rey and Jona were in the crowd, eagerly watching TWICE perform live. Jona was completely lost in the excitement of the concert, having no idea that Rey had spent the entire evening harboring a life-changing secret: he was planning to propose to her that very night.

Then, TWICE performed "YES or YES." As the song reached its final chorus, Rey leaned in close to Jona and began whispering the words softly into her ear. When she turned, he pulled out a small box, looked into her eyes, and asked the question one more time:

"YES or YES"

With the music thundering around them, Jona smiled and finally said yes.
