Our Story

Gerwel and Jane first met at Lyceum of Subic Bay. He was an IT student, she was studying Accountancy. Even before knowing each other’s names, they often crossed paths on the stairs, their eyes meeting in fleeting, unspoken moments. Gerwel had secretly admired Jane for almost a year, and after a memorable day with friends at Ayala Harbor Point in 2015, he finally found the courage to add her on Facebook. That was when their story truly began.

For a time, Jane stepped back, unsure and cautious, leaving both hearts aching. Life carried them along separate paths, until two years later fate brought them back together. Healed and stronger, their paused love story resumed. They faced challenges hand in hand, dreamed together, and built a life filled with laughter, love, and unwavering devotion. Now, they are here, ready to fulfill one of their most cherished dreams—uniting their hearts in marriage and beginning their Forever.

The Proposal

The proposal took place at Camp John Hay Baguio on January 27, 2024, at 11:45 AM Gerwel secretly hired a photographer who pretended to be a content creator and asked both of them for a quick 15-minute shoot. While Jane was being photographed, Gerwel quietly prepared the ring. He then knelt down behind her, with Yuki sitting sweetly beside him. When she turned, she was completely surprised. With a full heart, she said YES.
