How We Met

It started with a shared moment and a conversation that never seemed to end. Jona was captivated by Rey's quiet strength, while Rey found a home in Jona's radiant laughter.

Through seasons of growth, travel, and quiet afternoons, they discovered that their best versions were the ones they brought out in each other. What began as a simple meeting evolved into a beautiful partnership built on trust, respect, and an ever-deepening love.
