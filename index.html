Our Love Story

How It All Started

In October 2018 , it all started with something as simple as a reaction on social media.

We met on Facebook when he reacted to my story. Back then, reactions to stories would appear directly on Messenger, so I replied with a simple, "Napindot?"

Little did we know that those two words would become the beginning of a story filled with love, challenges, separation, finding our way back to each other, and countless moments of choosing one another again.

From that simple conversation, we continued talking until the day we finally met in person. Our first date was at McDonald's Dinalupihan—nothing extravagant, but it became the first page of our love story in real life.

After months of talking and getting to know each other, he asked if he could court me. And on December 1, 2018, we officially became a couple.

Finding Our Way Back

But just like any other relationship, our journey was not always easy.

After a year together, we went our separate ways for many reasons. Months passed without any communication, and we went on with our own lives. We thought that maybe our story had come to an end.

But sometimes, some stories are not meant to end in the first chapter.

One day, his brother, Kuya Arlee, would pick me up and bring me to their house. And through those simple moments, we slowly found our way back to each other. That was the beginning of our comeback—a new beginning filled with deeper understanding, stronger love, and a clearer reason to stay.

We went through so much together. There were happy days and difficult ones. There were moments when we wondered if we would ever make it this far.

But through it all, we chose to come back, forgive, understand, and love each other again.

Choosing Forever

And then, on July 1, 2026, after everything we had been through, he asked me the question that would change our story forever:

"Will you marry me?"

And I said "yes"

A simple question, but one that carried years of memories, challenges, heartbreak, second chances, and all the love that brought us back to each other.

From a simple "Napindot?" in 2018, to finding our way back to each other, and finally saying "Yes" to forever…

We have come a long way.

After all the challenges, the distance, the heartbreak, and the second chances…

We found our way back to each other.

And this time, we're choosing forever.
