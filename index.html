Our Story

How We Met

It started with a shared coffee and a conversation that never seemed to end. Jona was captivated by Rey's quiet strength, while Rey found a home in Jona's radiant laughter.

Through seasons of growth, travel, and quiet afternoons, we discovered that our best versions were the ones we brought out in each other. What began as a simple meeting evolved into a beautiful partnership built on trust, respect, and an ever-deepening love.

The Moment

The Proposal

"Under the starlit sky of our favorite coastal town, he asked, and she said 'Finally!'"

Rey planned a weekend getaway that felt like any other, but as the sun dipped below the horizon, he led Jona to a private terrace overlooking the ocean. With trembling hands and a heart full of promises, he got down on one knee. It wasn't just a question; it was the start of our forever.
